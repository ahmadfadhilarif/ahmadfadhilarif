# Number Grove

A 3D walking maths puzzle garden for the browser. Explore seven places (Adding Garden, Times Table Village, Odd and Even Pond, Match Maker Market, Windmill Hill, Number Bond Beach, Place Value Castle), each with 8 line-drawing puzzle panels that get harder as you go.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. It is a single self-contained file; it loads three.js (r128) from cdnjs and fonts from Google Fonts, so it needs an internet connection.

## Controls

- **Walk:** W A S D (Shift to run), or left thumb on a tablet
- **Look:** mouse or arrow keys, or drag on a tablet
- **Solve:** click a panel, click to start the line at the big circle, trace, click at the rounded end
- **Step back:** Esc, right-click, or the Step back button
- **Sit:** look at a bench and press E or click
