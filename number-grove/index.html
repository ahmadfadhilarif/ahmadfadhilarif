A walking maths puzzle garden

Number Grove

Explore a park, a village, a duck pond, a market, a windmill hill, a sandy beach and a castle, with rivers and gardens in between. Each place has 8 puzzle panels that start easy and get harder. Draw one line from the big circle to the rounded end so the numbers follow the rule. Follow the yellow cables to find the next panel, and solve them all to open each gate. Turn your sound on to hear the chickens, ducks and windmills, and look out for the little critters hopping about.

Walk
W A S D keys (Shift to run), or your left thumb on a tablet
Look
Move the mouse (arrow keys turn), or drag on a tablet
Solve
Click a panel, click again to start the line, move to trace it, then click at the rounded end
Step back
Esc, right-click, or the Step back button
Sit
Look at a bench and click (or press E) to sit. Press E again to stand.

How to play

Walk
W A S D keys. Hold Shift to run. Tablet: left thumb.
Look
Mouse, or the arrow keys to turn. Tablet: drag.
Solve
Look at a panel and click. Click once to start your line at the big circle, move the mouse to trace it, then click again when you reach the rounded end. Tablet: press the big circle and drag.
Power
A dark panel has no power. Follow the yellow cable from the last panel you solved.
Gates
Solve every panel in a place and its gate opens.
Owl
The owl flies to the next panel to solve, and a yellow glow runs along the cable to it.
