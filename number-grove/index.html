Places

From the easiest place to the hardest. Finish every panel in a place to open its gate to the next.

    How to play

    Draw one line from the big circle to the rounded end so the numbers follow the panel's rule. Follow the yellow cables to the next panel, and solve every panel in a place to open its gate.

    Walk
    W A S D keys (Shift to run), or your left thumb on a tablet
    Look
    Move the mouse (arrow keys turn), or drag on a tablet
    Solve
    Click a panel, click again to start the line, move to trace it, then click at the rounded end
    Step back
    Esc, right-click, or the Step back button
    Sit
    Look at a bench and click (or press E) to sit. Press E again to stand.
    Menu
    The Menu button, or press M

    The rules

    Ordering
    Touch every number, from the smallest to the biggest.
    Counting
    Touch the counting numbers in order. Stay off the others.
    Adding
    The numbers your line touches must add up to the target.
    Times tables
    Touch every number in the times table, and none of the others.
    Odd and even
    Your line is a fence. Keep odd and even numbers in different areas.
    Taking away
    Start at the top number and take away each number you touch, to land on the goal.
    Same answers
    Your line is a fence. Every area must hold sums (or doubles and halves) with the same answer.
    Number bonds
    Your line is a fence. The numbers in every area must add up to the target.
