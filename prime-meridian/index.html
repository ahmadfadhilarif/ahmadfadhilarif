Places

    How to play

    Draw one line from the big circle to the rounded end so the numbers follow the panel's rule. Follow the yellow cables from panel to panel. Finish the Welcome Garden and every place on the island opens: play them in any order, and if one is too tricky, try another. Each finished place charges its laser tower, which fires a beam to a receiver at the foot of Meridian Hill. When all nine beams arrive, the barrier in the caves inside the hill falls: solve the five challenges behind it and the lift takes you up to the rocket.

    The rules

    Stars
    In the Welcome Garden: touch every star, and keep off any clouds.
    Ordering
    Touch every number, from the smallest to the biggest.
    Counting
    Touch the counting numbers in order. Stay off the others.
    Adding
    The numbers your line touches must add up to the target.
    Times tables
    Touch every number in the times table, and none of the others.
    Odd and even
    Your line is a fence. Keep odd and even numbers in different areas.
    Taking away
    Start at the top number and take away each number you touch, to land on the goal.
    Same answers
    Your line is a fence. Every area must hold sums (or doubles and halves) with the same answer.
    Number bonds
    Your line is a fence. The numbers in every area must add up to the target.

    How to play

    Walk
    W A S D keys. Hold Shift to run. Tablet: left thumb.
    Look
    Mouse, or the arrow keys to turn. Tablet: drag.
    Solve
    Look at a panel and click. Click once to start your line at the big circle, move the mouse to trace it, then click again when you reach the rounded end. Tablet: press the big circle and drag.
    Power
    A dark panel has no power. Follow the yellow cable from the last panel you solved.
    Gates
    The Welcome Gate opens when you finish the Welcome Garden, and from then on every place's gates are open. A place's shortcut gate onto the meadow opens when you finish it.
    Meridian Hill
    Every spoke path leads into a cave under the hill. A finished place's laser tower fires at its receiver at the foot of the cliff. All nine beams bring down the barrier round the five final challenges in the middle of the cave. Solve those and the lift takes you up to the launch pad.
    Lanterns
    A lantern by each place's gates shows how far you have got: dark, amber or green.
    Owl
    The owl flies to the next panel with power near you, and a yellow glow runs along the cable to it.
